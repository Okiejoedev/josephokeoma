Questions

What business owners ask me

How much does it cost?

Templates are free. For a system built for your business, the price depends on the task, so I'll give you a clear quote after our free call, before any work starts. Where tools charge in dollars (AI, WhatsApp, hosting), I tell you upfront what they'll cost each month.

Do I or my staff need technical skills?

No. If your team can fill a form or send a WhatsApp message, they can use what I build. I set it up, show your team how it works, and stay available if anything breaks.

Is my business data safe?

Your data stays in accounts you own, like your Google Sheets or your database. I collect only what the task needs and follow Nigeria's Data Protection Act. You can ask me to delete anything at any time.

Will WhatsApp ban my number?

I build on Meta's official WhatsApp Business Platform, and only for business tasks like orders, bookings, support and payments, which Meta allows. A person can always take over the conversation.

What if the AI gets something wrong?

Every system I build has checks: totals are calculated with code, not guessed, and anything unclear goes to a person. We test it with your real data before it goes live.
